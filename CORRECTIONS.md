# Corrections to the version submitted for review

The documents in `paper/` are dated 11 October 2026. They differ from the version submitted on 9 October 2026,
which is frozen with the conference, in two places. Both are recorded here rather than silently applied.

**1. The set with prices learned instead of listed.** The submitted paper's limitations section said that with
prices learned as bands "few wholes were assembled and no roles formed". The first half holds: in 7 of the 8
worlds with signs almost nothing was assembled, from (0/6,870) to (43/5,354), with seed 311 the exception at
(1,215/1,215). The second half was wrong: roles did form, by product, in 4 of the 8 worlds with signs and in 0 of
the 8 twins (Fisher's one-sided exact test, p = 0.038). The sentence now reports that. The per-world table for
the set, which was in the submitted supplement and carries the numbers the correction rests on, is unchanged.

**2. The per-seed table for the cipher world at round 10,000.** The category columns of that one table had been
generated from readings of the record as it then stood rather than from the record cut at round 10,000, while its
caption named rounds 8,000–9,999. It is regenerated from the cut readings. The wholes and assembled columns were
already correct in every row; the accuracy columns move slightly, for instance seed 312 from 0.68 (0.21) to
0.63 (0.17), and the counts now come to what the paper states: roles formed in 7 of the 14 worlds with signs and
1 of the 14 twins. No number in the paper's text changed.

The supplement was also rewritten for this version: it describes the model, the measurements and every set in
full, for a reader who has not seen the paper, and is 58 pages where the submitted one was 44. No measurement
changed in the rewriting; the two corrections above are the only changes of fact.
